Vision and Mission: To emerge as an institution of excellence that imparts knowledge through education, learning, and research for the inclusive development of society. To provide technology-driven engineering education for societal needs, and foster professional skills.

ABOUT COLLEGE

The College of Engineering Chengannur commonly known as CEC, is an engineering institute in the state of Kerala, India, that was established by the Government of Kerala under the aegis of the Institute of Human Resources Development in 1993. The college is located in Chengannur, Alappuzha

COURSE OFFERED

The College of Engineering Chengannur (CEC) offers undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs in engineering and computer applicationsComputer Science and Engineering (CSE): Focuses on core computing, software development, and programming.CSE (Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning): Specialized branch covering smart systems, data science, and AI algorithms.Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE): Deals with electronic devices, communication systems, and microprocessors.Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE): Covers electrical machines, power systems, and control circuits.
