DIWALI

Celebrated between October and November, the festival lasts for five days. People clean their houses, make colorful rangoli designs on the floor, and light earthen oil lamps called diyas. Families pray to Goddess Lakshmi for wealth and prosperity, wear new clothes, and share sweets and gifts with friends and loved ones. The celebration primarily marks Lord Rama’s return to Ayodhya after 14 years in exile and his victory over the demon king Ravana, filling homes with joy, peace, and a sense of togetherness.
